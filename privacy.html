Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 July 2026

Nrutyapuri Dance Academy ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it when you visit nrutyapuri.in or buy tickets.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact details you provide — name, email address, and phone number — when buying a ticket or submitting an enquiry form.
  • Payment information is collected and processed directly by our payment partner, Razorpay. We do not see or store your card, UPI, or bank details.
  • Basic technical data (such as browser type) that your device sends automatically when visiting the site.

2. How we use your information

  • To process your ticket purchase and email your ticket number and confirmation.
  • To contact you regarding your booking, class enquiries, or important event updates.
  • To keep records of ticket sales and respond to support requests.

3. Sharing of information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with our payment processor (Razorpay) to complete transactions, and where required by law. Ticket confirmation emails are sent using standard email services.

4. Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. Payments are handled over secure, encrypted connections by Razorpay, a PCI-DSS compliant payment gateway.

5. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you by emailing us at nrutyapuridanceacademy@gmail.com.

6. Cookies

The site may store minimal data locally in your browser (such as your light/dark theme preference). We do not use tracking cookies for advertising.

7. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, contact us at nrutyapuridanceacademy@gmail.com or +91 87540 00520.
